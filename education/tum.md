# Technical University of Munich

Oct 2026 – Mar 2027 · Exchange semester · Munich

One semester of Master’s-level courses in Informatics: network security, deep learning, AI testing, a decentralised-systems lab and a seminar on AI regulation.

- Programme: Exchange semester in Informatics (Master’s courses), School of Computation, Information and Technology
- Home school: EURECOM, Diplôme d’Ingénieur, 3rd year
- Language: English
- Status: In progress

## Winter semester 2026/27 (5 courses)

### Network Security (IN2101) — Lecture + exercises
Security of communication networks, from the cryptographic building blocks up to the protocols that protect the Internet’s layers.
- Threats, attacker models and security goals
- Symmetric and asymmetric cryptography, hash functions, MACs
- Digital signatures, certificates and public-key infrastructures
- Authentication and key-exchange protocols, Kerberos
- Network-layer security: IPsec
- Transport-layer security: TLS
- Firewalls and intrusion detection
- Denial-of-service attacks and defences
- Wireless and link-layer security

### Introduction to Deep Learning (IN2346) — Prof. Matthias Nießner, Lecture + graded exercises
The foundations of deep learning, from linear models to modern architectures, with weekly PyTorch exercises.
- Machine-learning basics, linear and logistic regression
- Neural networks and backpropagation
- Optimisation: SGD, momentum, Adam, learning-rate schedules
- Training practice: initialisation, regularisation, dropout, batch normalisation
- Convolutional neural networks
- Recurrent networks and LSTMs
- Attention and transformers
- Autoencoders and generative models
- Exercises in Python and PyTorch

### AI Testing (CIT423002) — Prof. Andrea Stocco, Chair of Software Engineering for Data-intensive Applications
How to test systems whose behaviour is learned rather than written: what makes AI software fail, and how to find those failures systematically.
- What AI systems are and how they differ from conventional software
- Characteristics of AI systems relevant to testing
- Types of AI: machine learning, deep learning, generative models
- Data quality and its effect on model behaviour
- Bugs in AI models: taxonomy and root causes
- Testing deep neural networks in simulation
- Testing automated driving systems
- Testing LLM-based software

### Decentralized Information Systems Lab — Practical course (Praktikum)
A hands-on lab course on decentralised information systems, carried out as a team project over the semester. The project will be described here once it is under way.

### Seminar: the EU AI Act — Master’s seminar
A seminar on the European Union’s regulation of artificial intelligence: how it was made, what it covers, and how it classifies risk.
- The legislative process behind the AI Act
- Structure and scope of the regulation
- Risk levels: prohibited, high-risk, limited and minimal-risk systems
- Participatory approaches to AI governance
- Written paper and presentation
