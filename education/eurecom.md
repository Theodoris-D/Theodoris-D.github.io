# EURECOM

2024 – 2027 · Engineering school · Sophia Antipolis

The engineering degree (Master’s in Computer Science) at EURECOM, a graduate school in digital sciences. A first year of broad foundations, then the cybersecurity major: systems, networks, cryptography and machine learning.

- Degree: Diplôme d’Ingénieur (Master’s degree in Computer Science), cybersecurity major
- Years: 2024 – 2027; exchange at TUM in 2026/27
- Language: English and French
- Course pages: eurecom.fr/en/teaching ↗

## Spring 2026 (Cybersecurity major · 30 ECTS)

### Advanced Statistical Inference (ASI) — Spring 2026, Lectures + Python labs
Learning seen as probabilistic modelling of data, so that many classical methods become special cases of inference in more general models, with a focus on quantifying uncertainty.
- Linear algebra, calculus and probability recap
- Likelihood, prior, posterior; maximum likelihood
- Posterior estimation and model selection
- Variational inference and the Laplace approximation
- Markov chain Monte Carlo
- Bayesian linear regression and classification
- Gaussian processes
- Bayesian neural networks
- Gaussian mixtures, variational autoencoders, deep generative models

### IoT Communication Protocols (ProtIOT) — Spring 2026, Lectures + 2 labs
Low-power wide-area networks: how billions of cheap, battery-powered devices talk over long distances. Labs on Contiki-NG with the Cooja simulator, and a LoRaWAN network with ChirpStack.
- IoT concepts, lifecycle and device classes
- IEEE 802.15.4: physical and MAC layers, CSMA/CA
- 802.15.4e: time-slotted channel hopping; ZigBee
- 6LoWPAN: IPv6 over low-power networks, header compression
- LoRa and LoRaWAN: chirp spread spectrum, duty cycle, adaptive data rate
- SigFox
- Cellular IoT: LTE-M and NB-IoT
- Thread, Wi-SUN, IEEE 802.11ah

### Semester project: QEMU snapshot restore (Project) — Spring 2026, with Frédéric Kah
A benchmarking harness for high-frequency VM snapshot restore in QEMU, comparing five restore strategies for snapshot-based fuzzing.

### Reverse Engineering Malware (RevMal) — Spring 2026, Ghidra · x64dbg
Reverse engineering of Windows malware at the binary level, from how programs are compiled to how real malware hides from analysts.
- Software fundamentals, compilation, computer architecture
- Operating-system internals and the PE executable format
- x86 assembly, calling conventions, system calls
- Static and dynamic analysis in a grey-box approach
- Obfuscation, packing and anti-analysis techniques
- How real malware behaves and evades analysis

### Wireless Security (WiSec) — Spring 2026, Software-defined radio lab
The challenges of securing wireless communications, from the physical layer and modulations up to the application protocols.
- Introduction to wireless security
- Reverse engineering radio protocols
- Software-defined radio lab
- Bluetooth security
- Wi-Fi security

### IoT Application Protocols (AppIOT) — Spring 2026
The application protocols designed for constrained IoT devices, and the architectures that manage them.
- HTTP and why it struggles on constrained devices
- Client/server for IoT: CoAP, WebSocket
- Publish/subscribe: MQTT, XMPP, AMQP
- 3GPP architectures for IoT
- M2M, oneM2M and LwM2M device management

### Imaging Security (ImSecu) — Spring 2026, Lectures + labs
Image and video processing for security, with a focus on biometrics and person verification.
- Biometrics and person verification
- Lab: eigenfaces
- Digital watermarking, steganography, image forensics
- Lab: image integrity
- Video surveillance

### Project Management (ProjMan) — Spring 2026, Group case studies
Project-management concepts and techniques following the PMI areas, practised on case studies in groups.
- Scope, time and cost
- Quality
- Communication and organisation
- Risk

## Fall 2025 (Cybersecurity major · 30 ECTS)

### System and Network Security (SysSec) — Fall 2025, Capture-the-flag homework
A practice-oriented course on real attacks and their countermeasures across operating systems, networks and the web, taught to make students think like attackers. Homework takes the form of CTF challenges.
- History of system security
- Memory corruption, exploitation and modern countermeasures
- Trusted computing
- Windows and Unix security basics
- Race conditions
- Network security
- Web security
- Telephony fraud
- Malware and embedded-systems security
- Security certification of products

### Secure Communications (SecCom) — Fall 2025
An introduction to cryptography and to the security mechanisms built on it, from the mathematics of the primitives to authentication and public-key infrastructures.
- Security services and their placement in layered systems
- Classical ciphers; perfect vs computational secrecy; one-time pad
- Stream and block ciphers, modes of operation, AES
- Number theory; Diffie-Hellman, RSA, ElGamal, elliptic curves
- Hash functions, integrity, message authentication codes
- Digital signatures and non-repudiation
- Authentication protocols and key distribution, Kerberos
- Certificates and PKI

### Multiparty Computation and Blockchains (MPC) — Fall 2025, Project + exam
How several parties can compute together on private inputs without trusting each other, and the protocols behind blockchains.
- Simulation paradigm; semi-honest and malicious security
- Garbled circuits and two-party computation
- Oblivious transfer and commitments
- Secret sharing: additive, Shamir
- GMW and BGW protocols
- Compilers to malicious security, zero-knowledge proofs, coin tossing
- Blockchain foundations, Sybil attacks, the backbone protocol
- Proof of work and proof of stake

### Machine Learning and Intelligent Systems (MALIS) — Fall 2025, Lectures + labs
The core theory and families of machine-learning methods, and the practical problems of optimisation, overfitting and validation.
- Linear regression and classification
- Support vector machines
- Decision trees and ensemble learning
- Neural networks
- Learning theory, validation, overfitting, regularisation
- Clustering and k-means
- Dimensionality reduction and PCA

### Research Project (ResProj) — Fall 2025, with Prof. Melek Önen & Prof. Antonio Faonio
Verifiable secure aggregation for federated learning: PUDA extended to vectors, implemented in Rust.

### Software Development Methodologies (SoftDev) — Fall 2025
Development processes, version control, testing and team practices for building software.

### Image and Video Compression (ImCod) — Fall 2025
Transform coding, quantisation and the principles behind image and video codecs.

### Awareness-raising to Research (AwaRe) — Fall 2025
An introduction to how research is done, read and presented.

### Introduction to Management (ManagIntro) — Fall 2025
Organisations, strategy and the basics of management.

### Italian (A0) (ITA) — Fall 2025
Beginner Italian.
