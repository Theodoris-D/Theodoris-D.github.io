# CPGE MP2I / MPI

2022 – 2024 · Preparatory classes · Nantes

Two years of intensive preparatory classes (classes préparatoires) for the French engineering-school entrance exams. MP2I and MPI are the tracks built around mathematics and theoretical computer science, with physics alongside.

- School: Lycée Clemenceau, Nantes
- Tracks: MP2I (1st year), MPI (2nd year)
- Rhythm: About 35 hours of classes a week, weekly oral examinations (“colles”), written tests every week
- Outcome: Admission to EURECOM’s engineering programme

## Computer science (MP2I and MPI)

### Programming and algorithms — Both years, C and OCaml
Imperative programming in C and functional programming in OCaml, with proofs of correctness and complexity for every algorithm.
- Recursion, induction and loop invariants
- Complexity analysis
- Lists, stacks, queues, trees, heaps, hash tables
- Sorting and searching
- Divide and conquer, dynamic programming, greedy algorithms
- Graphs: traversals, shortest paths, spanning trees
- Memory management and pointers in C

### Theoretical computer science — MPI focus
The formal side of computing: what can be expressed, computed, and computed efficiently.
- Propositional and first-order logic, SAT
- Formal languages, regular expressions, finite automata
- Context-free grammars
- Decidability and the halting problem
- Complexity classes P and NP, reductions
- Concurrency and synchronisation
- Databases and SQL

## Mathematics (MP2I and MPI)

### Algebra — Both years
- Groups, rings, fields
- Polynomials
- Linear algebra: vector spaces, linear maps, matrices
- Determinants, eigenvalues, diagonalisation
- Euclidean spaces

### Analysis and probability — Both years
- Sequences and series of real numbers and functions
- Integration, power series
- Differential equations
- Topology of normed vector spaces
- Discrete probability, random variables

## Other subjects

### Physics and chemistry — Both years
Mechanics, electrical circuits, waves, thermodynamics, electromagnetism and chemistry.

### French and philosophy — Both years
Essay writing on a yearly theme drawn from literary and philosophical works.

### English — Both years
Written and oral English for the competitive exams.

### Supervised personal project (TIPE) — Both years
A small research project on a chosen topic, presented at the entrance exams.

Programmes summarised from the official national programmes for MP2I and MPI.
